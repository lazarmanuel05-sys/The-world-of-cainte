The Origin / 02

A personal idea.
Made real.

CAINTÈ began with the courage to turn a personal idea into something real. After years of wearing jewelry, Mathias had developed a clear vision of the pieces he wanted to wear: designs that could become a natural part of personal style. From that vision came CAINTÈ, with attention to shape, design, length, size and material down to the smallest detail.

“Is it something for me?”
“Is it too feminine?”
“Can I wear it?”

More than a colour.

For gold finishes, CAINTÈ uses Physical Vapor Deposition — PVD — to bond the finish to the steel surface and create a coating designed for everyday wear.
